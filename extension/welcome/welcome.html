This browser only. No account. No daily cap on Notion or Obsidian.

On a page

Select text, pick a color. Option+S highlights. Option+H opens the sidebar. Come back later and the marks are still there.

On a PDF

Open the PDF in Chrome, click the Inkmark icon, then Highlight this PDF. For files on disk, enable “Allow access to file URLs” on the extension card.

Obsidian

Connect Local REST API in Settings. Export writes the note into your vault. Folder picker and Markdown copy still work as fallbacks.

Notion

Connect Notion in Settings. Sign in, pick the pages Inkmark may use, then pick a destination. Unlimited exports.
