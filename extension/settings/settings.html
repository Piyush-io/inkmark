Highlighting

Shortcut Alt+S (Option+S on a Mac) highlights the selection in this color. Alt+H toggles the sidebar.

AI highlighting

Not connected.

Connect a model and Inkmark highlights the passages worth keeping, colored by kind: key point, evidence, definition, takeaway, caveat. Each highlight keeps a one-line reason as its note. The page’s text goes straight from this browser to the provider you pick, with your key.

One line per thing to look for. Reset to default

Firecrawl (optional): cleaner text from busy or script-heavy pages

Firecrawl fetches the page on its servers and returns just the article, so menus, comments and ads never reach the model. It can’t see pages behind your login. Get a key at firecrawl.dev.

Word, PowerPoint, Excel, EPUB and other files: open them in Inkmark’s document reader (converted on this computer with anydoc), then highlight by hand or with AI.

One-time OAuth app (needed for Sign in with Notion)

Create a public integration at Notion, paste this redirect URL, then paste the client ID and secret here. After that, Connect opens Notion’s Allow-access screen, same idea as Web Highlights.

Open Notion integrations. New integration → Public. Redirect URIs: paste the URL above.

Or paste an internal integration token

Faster if you do not want a public OAuth app. Create an Internal integration, share a page with it, paste the secret. Inkmark lists those pages so you never copy a page ID.
